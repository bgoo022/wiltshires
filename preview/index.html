Hawke's Bay bred

Practical Wiltshires for practical farms.

Goodwin Wiltshires is focused on sheep that fit real farming systems: strong shedding, sound structure, natural mothering and useful performance without adding more jobs to the calendar.

Selection is aimed at steady improvement rather than fashion — keeping the traits that make Wiltshires valuable while producing robust sheep that are straightforward to farm.
